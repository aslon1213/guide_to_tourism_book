HOMETASK
18. Answer the questions with a partner.
  1. What can a guest do if the air conditioning in the room is faulty?
  2. What is overbooking, and how can a hotel solve it?
  3. Which three phrases can you use to apologize to a guest?
  4. How do we form negative sentences in the Past Simple? Give an example with sleep.
  5. What is the difference between “Would you like…?” and “We could…” when offering solutions?
  6. When might a hotel offer a refund, and when a voucher?
  7. Why is it polite to use might when making a suggestion to a guest?
19. Write an online review (8–12 sentences) about a hotel or restaurant problem you experienced — real or imagined. Follow the guide.
  • Opening: where and when did you stay or eat? What did you book? (Past Simple)
  • The problem: describe at least TWO problems from this unit (leaking tap, noisy neighbours, cold meal, lost luggage…).
  • The reaction: what did you say, and how did the staff react? Did they apologize? What solution did they offer? (use a quote with could / would / might)
  • Closing: would you recommend the place? Why / why not?

20. Creative task: design an info card for new hotel staff called “Handling Complaints in 4 Steps” (listen → apologize → offer a solution → follow up). Write one example phrase for each step and decorate your card.

Step 1 — Listen: ____________________

Step 2 — Apologize: ____________________

Step 3 — Offer a solution: ____________________

Step 4 — Follow up: ____________________
