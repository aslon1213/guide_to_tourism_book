19. Answer the questions with a partner.
  1. What is the difference between a hostel and a resort?
  2. What facilities has your favourite hotel got?
  3. What is there in a typical hotel lobby?
  4. Which is cheaper in your city — a guesthouse or a chain hotel?
  5. What does a concierge do for guests?
  6. Describe your bedroom at home with three adjectives.
  7. What is the most luxurious hotel you know? What has it got?
20. Write a description of your perfect hotel (8–12 sentences). Use the plan to help you.
  • Sentence 1–2: name, location and type of hotel (boutique hotel, resort, guesthouse…)
  • Sentence 3–5: rooms and facilities with there is / there are
  • Sentence 6–8: features with has got / have got (Wi-Fi, air conditioning, spa…)
  • Sentence 9–10: compare it with a real hotel you know (cosier than, more modern than…)
  • Final sentence: finish with a superlative (the best, the most beautiful…)
