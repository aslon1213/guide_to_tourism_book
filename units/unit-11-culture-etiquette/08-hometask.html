HOMETASK
18. Answer the questions with a partner.
  1. What is the difference between a custom and a taboo? Give one example of each.
  2. When do we use must and when do we use should? Give a piece of advice for a visitor with each verb.
  3. What does mustn't mean? How is it different from shouldn't?
  4. Which phrases can you use to give your opinion politely? Name at least three.
  5. How can you disagree with a guest without sounding rude? Give two phrases.
  6. What does dress code mean, and where do tourists usually meet one?
  7. Why is hospitality important in tourism? What should a good host do?
19. Write a mini-article called “Mind Your Manners in My Country” (8–12 sentences). Follow the guided template. Use should, ought to, must and mustn't, at least two opinion phrases and six words from this unit.
  1. Visitors to my country sometimes feel confused because …
  2. First of all, when people meet, they usually …, so guests should …
  3. In homes here, you must …
  4. You mustn't …, because …
  5. At religious or sacred sites, the dress code is …
  6. If a host offers you …, you ought to …
  7. One topic that is taboo with strangers is …
  8. From my point of view, tipping here …
  9. Some people say our customs are strict, but I'm afraid I disagree because …
  10. In my opinion, the best advice for any visitor is …
20. Creative task: design a “Do's and Don'ts” pocket card that a hotel could give to its guests. Give the card a title and write four tips in each column. Use should / ought to on the left and mustn't on the right.

Card title:

✔ DO — You should…✘ DON'T — You mustn't…

  • I can express my opinion with phrases like “In my opinion” and “From my point of view”.

  • I can write a short etiquette guide for visitors to my country.
