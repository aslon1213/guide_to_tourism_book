Useful language — telephone phrases

On business calls we use polite, fixed phrases. They sound professional and are easy to understand, even on a bad line.

Answering the phone
  • Good morning, Sunrise Tours, Aziz speaking. How can I help you?
  • Reception, Malika speaking. What can I do for you?
Asking for somebody
  • Could I speak to Mr Karimov, please?
  • Could you put me through to extension 12, please?
Transferring and holding
  • One moment, please. I'll put you through.
  • Hold the line, please.
  • I'm afraid her line is engaged. Would you like to hold?
Ending the call
  • Is there anything else I can help you with?
  • Thank you for calling. Goodbye!

Useful language — spelling and clarifying information

Names and numbers cause most telephone mistakes. Spell them with international code words and always check what you hear.

Asking for and giving spelling
  • Could you spell that for me, please?
  • That's S for Sierra, O for Oscar, T for Tango, O for Oscar.
Clarifying when you don't understand
  • Sorry, I didn't catch that. Could you repeat it, please?
  • Did you say 15 or 50?
  • Could you speak up a little? The line is bad.
  • Let me check: that's K-A-R-I-M-O-V, is that right?

Useful language — taking notes and messages

When you take a message, note only the key words and numbers, then read everything back to the caller to check it.

Offering to take a message
  • Can I take a message?
  • Would you like to leave a message?
Getting the details
  • Could I have your name and number, please?
  • And what is the message about?
Checking and promising action
  • Let me read that back to you.
  • I'll pass your message on to the manager.
  • She'll call you back as soon as possible.
