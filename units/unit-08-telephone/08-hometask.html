19. Answer the questions with a partner.
  1. What do you say when you answer a business call at a tour company?
  2. What does “put through” mean?
  3. Which code words do we use for the letters B, V and Z?
  4. What information must you write on a message pad?
  5. What do you say when the line is bad and you cannot hear the caller?
  6. How do you check a phone number that you are not sure about?
  7. How do you end a business call politely?
20. Write out a complete phone call (8–12 lines) in which a customer calls a tour company to book a day trip to the Chimgan Mountains. Follow the plan below and use at least six phrases from this unit.
  • The agent answers the phone professionally.
  • The caller says what they want to book and for which day.
  • The agent asks for the caller's name; the caller spells it with code words.
  • The caller asks about the pick-up time; the agent gives the details.
  • The agent checks a number with a clarifying question and reads everything back.
  • They end the call politely.

21. Creative task: design a “While you were out” message card for a hotel reception desk. Then fill it in with the message from the listening on page 68.

Your card needs spaces for: who the message is for · date and time · the caller's name and number · the message · what action is needed · who took the message.
