18. Answer the questions with a partner.
  1. What is the difference between a discount and a special offer?
  2. What does all-inclusive mean? What is usually included in the price?
  3. Which three persuasive adjectives would you use to describe your favourite destination, and why?
  4. How do promoters use the First Conditional to sell? Give your own example offer.
  5. Why do adverts use superlatives like "the best" and "the most beautiful"? Should customers always believe them?
  6. What does limited availability mean, and why does it make people book faster?
  7. Which phrases can you use to call a customer to action? Name at least three.

  7. If you book before …, you will …
