WARM UP
1. Discuss these questions in pairs. Be ready to share your answers with the class.
  • What makes you choose one hotel or tour over another — the price, the photos, the reviews, or something else?
  • Where do you usually see advertisements for holidays? Which ones catch your eye, and why?
  • Can you remember an advert that made you really want to travel somewhere? What exactly made it so persuasive?
