Useful language — persuading customers

Rule of use: good promotion follows a path — paint a picture, show the value, create urgency, and finish with a clear call to action. In that order.

Grabbing attention
  • Imagine waking up to the sound of waves.
  • Are you dreaming of a real adventure?
  • Don't miss the experience of a lifetime.
Emphasizing value
  • All this for just $299 per person.
  • That's a saving of $80 per couple!
  • Children under six stay absolutely free.
Creating urgency
  • Hurry — only five places left!
  • This offer ends on Sunday at midnight.
  • Availability is strictly limited.
Calling to action
  • Book today and start packing!
  • Call us now on +998 71 200 00 00.
  • Visit our website and reserve your place.

You met the First Conditional in Unit 5, where waiters used it for service. Here it returns as a selling tool: the if-clause names the customer's action, and the will-clause promises a reward.

First Conditional — making sales offers

When do we use it? In promotion, the First Conditional links a customer action (if + Present Simple) to a promised reward (will + base verb). It makes the offer sound real and immediate — perfect for closing a sale.

  • If you book today, you will get a 10% discount.
  • If you stay five nights, we will upgrade you to a sea-view room.
  • You won't pay for the transfer if you reserve online.
  • Will we get the early-bird price if we pay in advance?

In Unit 2 you used comparatives and superlatives to describe hotels. Promoters use the same forms to make their product the obvious choice — often strengthened with intensifiers: far cheaper, much more spacious, by far the best.

Comparatives and superlatives — the promoter's favourite tools

When do we use them? Use the comparative + than to show your offer beats the competition, and the + superlative to crown it the number-one choice. At B2 level, strengthen your claims with intensifiers: far / much + comparative, by far / easily + superlative, or the most … ever.

  • Our private beach is far cleaner than any city pool.
  • A night in a yurt is much more memorable than another hotel stay.
  • This is by far the most popular tour we have ever offered.
  • Book direct — you will get a better rate than on any booking site.
