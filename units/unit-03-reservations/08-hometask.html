HOMETASK
18. Answer the questions with a partner.
  1. What three pieces of information do you usually need to make a hotel reservation?
  2. What is the difference between a deposit and a refund?
  3. When do we use will — and when is going to better?
  4. Which future form do we use for fixed arrangements with a date and time?
  5. What does a cancellation policy tell you?
  6. Why are rates higher in the peak season?
  7. What should you do if you don't receive a confirmation email after booking?
19. Write 8–12 sentences with the title “A trip I am going to take”. Describe a real or imaginary trip you are planning.

Include:

  • two plans with going to (where? with whom? how long?);
  • two fixed arrangements in the Present Continuous with dates or times (flights, check-in, meetings);
  • two predictions or promises with will;
  • at least five vocabulary words from this unit (deposit, rate, confirmation, availability, cancellation policy, refund, invoice…).
20. Creative task: design a one-page booking information card for a small guesthouse in your town.

Your card must show: the guesthouse name · nightly rates for the peak and low season · the deposit you charge · your cancellation policy · the payment methods you accept · when the guest will receive the confirmation and booking reference. Add a small picture or logo if you like. Be ready to present your card in the next lesson.

Self-check: tick what you can do now.
  • I can use will for offers, promises and predictions.
  • I can talk about plans with going to and fixed arrangements with the Present Continuous.
  • I can make, change and cancel a reservation in English.
  • I can ask about availability, rates, deposits and cancellation policies.
  • I can write a short confirmation email to a customer.
