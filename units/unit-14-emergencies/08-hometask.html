18. Answer the questions with a partner.
  1. What is the difference between a fire drill and a real evacuation?
  2. Name five pieces of safety equipment a hotel must have and say where each one is kept.
  3. How do we form the negative imperative? Give two examples from this unit.
  4. Complete the warning: “If you block the emergency exit, …”
  5. Which tense do we use in incident reports, and why?
  6. What did Madina Yusupova do when the laundry-room fire occurred? (Look back at the reading.)
  7. What will you do first if a guest faints in the lobby?
19. Write a full incident report (8–12 sentences) about an imaginary accident at a hotel or tourist site. Use the Past Simple and the formal verbs from this unit, and finish with one First Conditional recommendation.

Follow this template:

  • Date and time: when did the incident occur?
  • Location: where exactly did it happen?
  • People involved: who was there? Who witnessed it?
  • What occurred: describe the events in order.
  • How staff responded: who called whom? Who gave first aid?
  • Injuries and damage: was anyone injured? Was anything broken?
  • Recommendation: “If we … , similar accidents will not occur again.”
