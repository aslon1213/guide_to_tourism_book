18. Imagine you have just checked in at a hotel in another country. Write a blog post of 8–12 sentences about your arrival. Use the plan to help you.
  • Where have you just arrived? What is the hotel called?
  • What did the receptionist politely ask you for? (passport, registration form, deposit…)
  • What has the porter or receptionist already done for you?
  • What have you already done — and what haven't you done yet?
  • One polite request you made, in the receptionist's exact words or yours.

Use at least five Present Perfect forms and five vocabulary items from this unit.
