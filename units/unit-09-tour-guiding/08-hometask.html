HOMETASK
18. Answer the questions with a partner.
  1. What is the difference between a day trip and a package tour?
  2. Which sequencing words can you use to explain a tour plan? Name at least four.
  3. When do we use the Past Continuous instead of the Past Simple? Give an example from a tour.
  4. What is a rhetorical question, and why do guides use them?
  5. What does a guide do during a headcount, and when should they do it?
  6. Which phrases can a guide use to invite the group's attention to a small detail?
  7. What makes a guide's commentary interesting — facts, stories, or both? Why?
19. Write the story of a memorable tour (8–12 sentences) — one you joined, led, or imagine. Follow the guided template. Use the Past Simple, the Past Continuous and at least four sequencing markers.
  1. Two years ago / Last summer, I joined (or led) a … to …
  2. Our guide, …, met us at … and …
  3. First of all, …
  4. Then, while we …, …
  5. When we arrived, …
  6. After that, …
  7. While we were …, something surprising happened: …
  8. Next, the guide pointed out …
  9. Finally, …
  10. I will always remember … because …
20. Creative task: design an itinerary card for a half-day walking tour of your own town. Give the tour a name, complete the table, and write two or three sentences below it using sequencing words and one rhetorical question.
